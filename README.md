# Connect Human

Build Icebreaker — A Premium Event Networking Experience

You are an award-winning product designer and senior frontend engineer.

Your task is to build a premium frontend-only web application called Icebreaker.

This is NOT a CRUD application.

This is NOT a dashboard.

This is NOT another networking app.

It is a carefully designed product experience.

Every animation, transition and interaction should feel intentional.

The final product should look like something designed by Apple, Linear or Arc Browser—not something generated by AI.

Everything is frontend only.

No backend.

No database.

No authentication.

No API.

Everything is hardcoded with believable dummy data.

The app should feel completely real even though nothing actually connects to a server.

The Product

Icebreaker helps people discover who they should meet before starting a conversation.

Imagine arriving at a hackathon with 200 strangers.

Normally networking is random.

Icebreaker removes the randomness.

You discover people who share your interests, skills, goals and projects before saying hello.

The product isn't about finding everyone.

It's about finding the RIGHT people.

The product should communicate this visually without requiring lots of text.

Discovery Experience

This is the most important part.

DO NOT build a list.

DO NOT build a dashboard.

DO NOT build cards immediately.

The user presses

Discover People

The interface becomes almost empty.

Dark background.

A single pulse expands across the screen.

As the pulse travels...

People gradually emerge.

Not by fading in.

Instead...

Imagine photographs slowly developing.

Each person gently appears from darkness.

The interface should create curiosity.

Once everyone has appeared...

The layout reorganises itself.

One person slowly moves forward.

Everyone else softly fades into the background.

The Goal

When someone watches this demo for the first time, they should immediately think:

"I want to use this at my next conference."

The product should feel so polished that people forget it is only a frontend prototype.

Prioritise exceptional interaction design over feature count.

If there is a choice between adding another feature or making one interaction unforgettable, always choose the unforgettable interaction.
